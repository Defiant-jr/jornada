Welcome to Site Meter Signup
Site Meter offers two primary website and blog counter-tracking packages. Site Meter Basic provides all the essential data, statistics, and reports needed to know who is visiting your site, how many pages have been viewed, and detailed information about each individual visitor. Site Meter Premium offers additional statistics including ranked reports, more historical data, ad-free stats pages, and the ability to export data on recent visitors. For a full comparison of features visit our Product Comparison page.
